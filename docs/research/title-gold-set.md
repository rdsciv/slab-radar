## 3. Labeling spec

### 3.1 Card key and fields
`card_key = (year, manufacturer, product, subset, card_no, parallel, is_auto, variation)`, resolved to a `dim_card` row, which carries `gemrate_id` when available. Print run is a **check**, not a key component: it is implied by the parallel. A title print run that disagrees with the catalog → flag.

`grade_key = (grader, grade, grade_label, qualifier)`. Examples:
- `PSA|10|GEM MINT|-`
- `PSA|8|NM-MT|OC`
- `BGS|10|Black Label|-` ≠ `BGS|10|Pristine|-`
- `SGC|10|Pristine|-` ≠ `SGC|10|GEM MINT|-`
- `CGC|10|Perfect|-` ≠ `CGC|10|Pristine|-` ≠ `CGC|10|GEM MINT|-`
- `TAG|10|Pristine|-` ≠ `TAG|10|GEM MINT|-`
- raw = `RAW|-|-|-`
- authentic-only = `<grader>|AUTH|-|-`

When the label is absent: `SGC 10` with no word is labeled `grade_label=null`. The resolver then maps SGC 10 with a null label to GEM MINT only if the comp source or cert lookup confirms it. Otherwise it gets `grade_label_uncertain=true` and is **excluded from Pristine-vs-Gem price stats**, though it stays in a combined "SGC 10 any" bucket. The same rule applies to CGC 10, BGS 10, and TAG 10. Each grader's Pristine population is a small minority and sells at a premium, so a silent default would contaminate the Gem series.

`subgrades`: an object with `centering/corners/edges/surface` only when the title labels them or uses canonical `C/Co/E/S` order or a 4-slash string. Space-separated unlabeled values → `{"unordered":[…]}`.

`auto_grade`: second number in `10/10` (SGC), `9.5/10` (BGS), `Auto 10` (PSA/DNA).

`tag_score`: integer 100–1000 when present, e.g. `(895)`.

`exclusion_reason`: one of `multi_card_lot | sealed_product_lot | you_pick | player_unspecified | aftermarket_auto | hype_raw | reprint | custom | digital | not_a_card | wrong_product`. When it's set, card_key fields may be partially filled, but the record is not a sale of a single tracked card.

### 3.2 Ambiguity labeling (labelers mark; parser must reproduce)
A title is **AMBIGUOUS** (`ambiguous=true`, with a reason) when any of these holds:
1. **Missing card number with more than one catalog candidate** in (player, year, product, parallel, is_auto). Example: Shough 2025 Prizm Silver with no number → #327 and #429 both exist (GemRate: Silver Prizm #327, n=152; #429, n=193). Other examples: Mosaic Reactive Blue #273/#312 (seed-041), Select Zebra #41/#141/#341/#441.
   - *Resolvable* when only one candidate exists. Examples: Shough Lazer, Blue Wave /230, and Silver auto exist only at #327 (seed-005/014/017). Label the resolved card_no and add the hazard `no_card_no_resolved_by_catalog`.
   - *Variation words resolve #429*: "Rookie Variation", "Image Variation", "Variation", "RV", "SP Variation" (seed-001/002).
2. **Parallel unstated or unresolvable**: a bare "Prizm" (seed-011), "Green Pulsar" vs "Neon Green Pulsar" (seed-075), or a "Gold Auto /10" with two /10 gold candidates (seed-076).
3. **Year missing** (seed-113, 123) or contradictory (two year tokens, neither adjacent to the product).
4. **Card number conflicts with the catalog** (seed-053: "#19" vs GemRate `RW-TSH`).
5. **Grade unparseable or self-contradictory**: `PSA 10 OC` (no qualified 10s; syn-044), grade without grader (syn-058).
6. **Source integrity conflict**: the title disagrees with the URL slug or cert lookup (seed-098/099).

An ambiguous record **never** gets a card_key in production. It goes to the Review Queue. In the gold set, labelers fill in what is certain and leave the rest null.

### 3.3 What counts as "correct" (precision)
Unit: one title. Parser output for a title is one of:
- (a) `card_key + grade_key` (an assignment)
- (b) `REVIEW` (ambiguous or low confidence)
- (c) `EXCLUDE(reason)`

- **Precision** (the ≥98% target) = correct assignments ÷ all assignments, counted over titles where the parser output (a). Correct means all of these hold:
  - card_key equals gold exactly. Year, product, subset, card_no, parallel, and is_auto all match; for gold-ambiguous titles, any assignment counts as wrong.
  - grade_key equals gold exactly, including grade_label (Pristine vs Gem vs Black Label) and qualifier.
  - Print run, serial, subgrades, and tag_score are scored separately as secondary field accuracy and do not enter the precision gate.
- An assignment on a gold-EXCLUDED title is a **false positive**: it counts in the precision denominator as wrong.
- **Coverage** (secondary) = assignments ÷ titles that gold says are assignable. Target ≥85% in M2. Low coverage is acceptable because it routes to review, while low precision corrupts prices.
- **Exclusion recall** = parser excludes ÷ gold excludes. Target ≥95%. A lot or reprint priced as a single slab is the worst error.
- Report precision overall, and per stratum for product family, grader, and source.

### 3.4 Inter-rater rules
- Two independent labelers on 100% of the first 100 titles, then 25% double-labeled. Labelers can be the user plus a Claude labeler run with this spec. An LLM labeler never adjudicates.
- Agreement is measured on the composite `card_key`, the composite `grade_key`, and `exclusion_reason`, using Cohen's κ on (assign / ambiguous / exclude) and exact match on the keys.
- Gate: κ ≥ 0.85 and key exact-match ≥ 95% on the double-labeled set. Below that, fix this spec and relabel. Don't average.
- Disagreements are adjudicated by the user (business owner). The rule that resolves each one gets added to §3.2 and `docs/lessons.md`.
- Labelers may consult the catalog (GemRate CSV, checklists) and the listing image when available. They **may not** use price to infer the parallel.
- Labelers record `evidence` (`title_only | catalog | image | cert_lookup`). Gold labels that need image evidence are flagged, because the parser cannot be expected to get them from the title. They're kept in the set and expected to route to REVIEW.

### 3.5 Sampling plan to ≥300 (and why 300 is a floor, not a proof)
Wilson 95% score interval:

| n | correct | point | Wilson 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|
| 300 | 294 | 98.0% | **[95.7%, 99.1%]** |
| 300 | 298 | 99.3% | [97.6%, 99.8%] |
| 300 | 299 | 99.7% | [98.1%, 99.9%] |
| 300 | 300 | 100% | [98.7%, 100%] |
| 1000 | 980 | 98.0% | [96.9%, 98.7%] |

At n=300, an observed 98% cannot rule out a true 95.7%. To claim "precision ≥98% with 95% confidence" (Wilson lower bound ≥98%), you need 0 errors in ≥188 assignments, ≤1 in ≥279, ≤2 in ≥360, or ≤3 in ≥436.

**Proposed gate:**
- Point precision ≥98% **and** Wilson LB ≥96% on ≥300 *assigned* titles. That allows ≤6 errors at n=322 or ≤2 at n=178.
- Plus an LB ≥96% check on the core stratum (Prizm, Optic, and Topps Chrome flagship RCs for watchlist players), which means ~140 assigned core titles with ≤1 error.

Titles routed to review don't count as assigned, so **sample ~400 titles** to land ≥300 assignments at the expected 75–85% coverage.

## 4. Rules-first parser design

### 4.1 Ordered stages
Each stage consumes tokens and records the evidence span and confidence it produced.

0. **Pre-normalize.** NFKC, strip emoji and repeated punctuation, collapse whitespace, uppercase for matching while keeping the original. Strip site suffixes: `for sale online`, `on Fanatics Collect`, `- Walmart.com`, `: Everything Else`, a trailing `...`. Normalize `&`→`AND`, `/`-seasons to `-`, `#/99`→`/99`. Split glued tokens (`BGS9`→`BGS 9`, `10🔥RC`→`10 RC`).
1. **Exclusion screen** (short-circuit to EXCLUDE). Applies before anything else, because a lot title often contains valid card tokens.
   - `\bLOT\b|\bLOT OF\b|^\(\d+\)\s|\bx\d+\b|\b\d+\s*CARD LOT\b`
   - `YOU PICK|PICK YOUR|COMPLETE YOUR SET|CHOOSE`
   - `REPRINT|\bRP\b|CUSTOM|ACEO|FAN ART|NOVELTY|PROXY`
   - `PSA READY|GEM\?|POTENTIAL 10|PRE-?GRADE|SHOULD GRADE` (only when no grader+number is present)
   - `NFT|DIGITAL|TOPPS BUNT|PANINI ADRENALYN DIGITAL`
   - `IRL AUTO|IN PERSON AUTO|SIGNED IN PERSON|\bIP AUTO\b|FACSIMILE`: this marks the auto as non-certified, not a full exclude, unless the title claims a certified auto
   - `PACK|BOX|BLASTER|HOBBY BOX|SEALED` without a card number
   - `EMPTY SLAB|CASE ONLY`
   - Two or more distinct grader+grade pairs → multi_card_lot
   - Two or more full player names from the roster with no known multi-player subset → multi_card_lot
2. **Grader/grade.** One regex per grader, label-before or label-after:
   - PSA: `\bPSA(?:/DNA)?\s*(?:(GEM\s*MI?N?T|GEM MT|MINT|NM-?MT|NM|EX-?MT)\s*)?(10|[1-9](?:\.5)?)\s*(?:(GEM\s*MI?N?T|GEM|MINT\+?|NM-?MT\+?)\b)?\s*\(?\b(OC|ST|PD|MC|MK|OF)\b\)?`
   - BGS: `\b(BGS|BECKETT)\s*(?:(PRISTINE|BLACK\s*LABEL|GEM\s*MINT)\s*)?(10|9\.5|[1-9](?:\.5)?)`, plus `BLACK\s*LABEL`, plus subgrades `(\d{1,2}(?:\.5)?)/(…)/(…)/(…)`, or `C\s*(\d+(?:\.5)?)\s*CO\s*(…)\s*E\s*(…)\s*S\s*(…)`, or a parenthesized group of 4 numbers.
   - SGC: `\bSGC\s*(10|[1-9](?:\.5)?)\s*(PRI(?:STINE)?|GEM\s*MINT|MINT\+?)?(?:/(10|[1-9]))?`. The trailing `/N` is auto_grade.
   - CGC: `\bCGC\s*(?:(PRISTINE|PERFECT|GEM\s*MINT)\s*)?(10|[1-9](?:\.5)?|AUTH(?:ENTIC)?)\s*(PRISTINE|PERFECT|GEM\s*MINT|NM-?MT\+?|MINT\+?)?`
   - TAG: `\bTAG\s*(?:(PRISTINE|GEM\s*MINT)\s*)?(10|[1-9](?:\.5)?)\s*(?:\((\d{3,4})\))?`
   - Ranges are validated: PSA has no .5 at 10 and no qualifier on 10; BGS/SGC/CGC/TAG allow .5. Subgrades must be consistent with the overall grade: BGS overall ≤ min(subgrades)+1, otherwise lower the confidence. Any consumed grade number is removed from the token stream so it can't be misread as a card number (seed-129 `#10 BGS 10`).
3. **Serial / print run.** Serial first: `#?\(?\s*#?(\d{1,4})\s*/\s*(\d{1,4})\)?`, accepted when n ≤ d and d is in the product's set of print runs. That guards against grade pairs like `10/10`, which were consumed in stage 2. Then a bare `/(\d{1,4})\b` is the print run. A leading `#` on a serial must not become a card number (seed-054).
4. **Year.** All 4-digit years and season patterns. Pick the year adjacent (±3 tokens) to the product alias. More than one non-adjacent year → AMBIGUOUS.
5. **Product + subset.** Longest-match over the product alias dictionary (§4.2), ordered longest first: `DONRUSS OPTIC PREVIEW` before `DONRUSS OPTIC` before `OPTIC` before `DONRUSS`, and `PRIZM DRAFT PICKS`/`PRIZM DP` before `PRIZM`. Then subset aliases scoped to the product (Manga Vertical, Prizmatic, Light It Up, Rated Rookie, Rookie Variation, RPA…).
6. **Card number.** `#\s*([A-Z]{1,5}-?[A-Z0-9]{1,5}|\d{1,4})`, `\bNO\.?\s*(\d{1,4})`, `\bRC\s+(\d{2,4})\b`, and bare alphanumeric codes matching the product's prefix pattern (`CRPA-`, `DPA-`, `RA-`, `RGK-`). The candidate must exist in the catalog for (player, year, product). If it doesn't → hazard `card_no_conflicts_with_catalog` and confidence drops.
7. **Parallel.** Product-scoped dictionary match, longest match first, then **negative near-miss check**: if "SILVER" (Prizm) / "HOLO" (Optic) / "REFRACTOR" (Topps Chrome) matched but any head-word from that product's *other-parallel* list is also present, discard the generic match and keep the specific one. No parallel token → `Base`, at confidence 0.85. A bare `PRIZM` in a Prizm title → ambiguous between Base and Silver.
8. **Auto/patch.** Token set (§1.6). Rule out facsimile/IRL.
9. **Player.** Roster-anchored full-name match (dim_player aliases), with an apostrophe and suffix-tolerant normalizer: `A JA`→`A'JA`, `JR.`/`II`. Surname-only matches are rejected.
10. **Catalog resolution.** Query dim_card with the extracted fields. Exactly 1 candidate → assign. 0 → REVIEW (`no_catalog_match`). More than 1 → AMBIGUOUS. Variation words act as a filter: they keep #401+ variation rows in Prizm and drop them otherwise.
11. **Validation.**
    - The print run must match the parallel's print run.
    - An auto must match an auto row.
    - The grade must be valid for the grader.
    - The year must be within the product's release years.
    - is_rc is never taken from the title.

### 4.3 Confidence scoring
`conf = Π stage_conf × catalog_factor`. Stage defaults:

| Signal | Confidence |
|---|---|
| Explicit `#card_no` that exists in the catalog | 1.0 |
| card_no resolved by unique-candidate | 0.95 |
| Explicit parallel token, no near-miss words | 1.0 |
| Default-Base | 0.85 |
| Bare PRIZM | 0.5 |
| Grader + number + label | 1.0 |
| Grader + number, no label, where Pristine is possible | 0.9 (grade_label_uncertain) |
| Year adjacent to the product | 1.0 |
| Year non-adjacent and unique | 0.95 |
| Print run matches the catalog | ×1.0 |
| Print run mismatch | ×0.5 |
| Print run absent | ×1.0 |
| Any `hazard` from the high-risk list (stray year, slug conflict, unknown trailing token) | ×0.9 each |

Routing:
- conf ≥ 0.95 → auto-assign.
- 0.80–0.95 → assign with a `low_conf` flag, excluded from headline stats until the gold precision for that band is ≥98%.
- < 0.80 → LLM fallback (below).
- AMBIGUOUS (stage 10 found more than one candidate) → Review Queue directly. **The LLM is not allowed to break catalog ties that the title doesn't resolve.**

Calibrate the thresholds on the gold set: choose the auto-assign threshold as the lowest value at which per-band precision has a Wilson LB ≥96%.

### 4.4 LLM fallback
- When: rules confidence < 0.80 **and** not stage-10 AMBIGUOUS **and** not excluded. Typical cases are missing or oddly ordered tokens, unknown inserts, typos (`Varient`), and new parallels not yet in the alias table. Expected volume after M2 tuning is ~5–10% of titles.
- How: a small Claude model with structured JSON output that matches the §3.1 field schema. The prompt includes the candidate rows from dim_card for (player, year, product), so the model **selects** a candidate id or `"none"` and never invents a card. Temperature 0.
- Post-validation: re-run stage 11 on the LLM output. The selected candidate must exist, and its tokens must be supported by the title: each field value must align to a title span, or else stay null. Any validation failure → REVIEW.
- Cache by normalized title hash. Log the LLM decision and its evidence spans. LLM-resolved assignments are reported as a separate precision stratum in the gold evaluation.

## 5. Top parsing hazards observed (real titles)
1. **"Silver" belongs to a different parallel or product.**
   - `Silver Lazer` = Lazer Prizm (4 of 20 Shough Prizm-product titles in the seed).
   - In Optic, `Silver Holo` = Holo.
   - `Silver Seismic` = Phoenix.
   - `Fireworks Silver` = an insert.
   - `Light It Up … Silver Prizm` = a Mosaic insert.
2. **The card number is missing while the catalog has several candidates.**
   - Shough Silver is #327 or #429.
   - Mosaic Reactive is #273 or #312.
   - Select Zebra is #41/#141/#341/#441.
   - Other missing-number cases resolve uniquely through the catalog: Lazer, Blue Wave, and the Silver auto all exist only at #327.
3. **Product-name collisions.**
   - "Optic Preview" is the Donruss product, not Optic.
   - "Prizm Draft Picks" and "No Huddle" are both separate products and Prizm parallel names.
   - "Mosaic" is both a product and a Prizm parallel.
   - "Chrome" appears inside Panini titles.
4. **Grade-string variety.**
   - Label before the number (`PSA GEM MT 10`, `BGS PRISTINE 10`).
   - Glued tokens (`BGS9`).
   - `SGC 10/10` is a card grade plus an auto grade.
   - Unlabeled, unordered subgrades.
   - Raw condition words with no grader (`MINT`, `NRMT+`).
   - `CGC AUTH`.
   - Pristine vs Gem vs Black Label is missing from many titles.
5. **Source-integrity and noise tokens.**
   - Goldin `(17)` lot prefixes.
   - Stray years (`… SAINTS 2017`) and trailing SKUs (`PSA 9 112`, `GS1`).
   - Serials carrying `#`.
   - Site suffixes in search-indexed titles.
   - Fanatics URLs recycled for a different card than the title.
